How It Works

RupeeCoin in four steps.

A closed-loop community currency with simple, transparent rules anyone can follow.

01
Create your wallet

Anyone can register a wallet name and PIN. You receive a 1,000 RC genesis grant instantly — no purchase, no approval needed.

02
Send & receive

Transfer RC to any wallet by name. Confirm with your PIN. The transaction is queued and sealed into the next block.

03
Blocks seal transactions

Every 59 seconds a new block is mined. All pending transfers are confirmed, the chain extends, and block height increments.

04
Fixed supply

When 21,000,000 RC is reached, no more can ever be created. The genesis grant closes; the network continues on existing supply.
